## What I learnt

Basic understanding of the machine learning and neural networks.

AI & ML Foundations: Machine Learning operates as a specialized subset of Artificial Intelligence designed to learn patterns from data rather than relying on explicit rules.

Problem Suitability: Before applying ML, determine if the problem is deterministic. If a deterministic formula or rule-based logic already solves the problem efficiently, ML is unnecessary.

Neural Networks & Deep Learning: Neural Networks are a subfield of ML. Architectures containing three or more hidden layers transition into the Deep Learning domain to model complex, non linear relationships.
